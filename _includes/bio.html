Olivier Côté is a Ph.D. candidate in Actuarial Science at Université Laval and an Associate of the Casualty Actuarial Society. Supervised by Marie-Pier Côté (Université Laval) and Arthur Charpentier (UQAM), his thesis takes a causal perspective on direct and indirect discrimination in insurance predictive models, in collaboration with one of the largest P&C insurers in Canada. His research on fairness in insurance pricing has appeared in the Journal of Risk and Insurance and in Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, and his scalable toolbox for exposing indirect discrimination in insurance rates, published by the Casualty Actuarial Society, won the American Academy of Actuaries 2026 Award for Research. His doctoral work is supported by an NSERC Canada Graduate Research Scholarship and the SOA Hickman Scholars Program.
