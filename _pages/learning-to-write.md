<p class="ltw-lede">I am still learning to write. This page is a notebook of the books, essays and lines that have helped me so far: things I go back to when a draft feels stuck. It is not advice; it is a record of what is working for me, and it will keep changing.</p>

## What I keep relearning

<ul class="ltw-notes">
  <li><strong>I write to find out what I think.</strong> My first draft is for me; the later ones are for the reader.</li>
  <li><strong>I forget what the reader does not know.</strong> When a section feels obvious to me, it usually needs one more sentence.</li>
  <li><strong>I cut more than I add.</strong> The paragraph I like most is often the one the paper does not need.</li>
  <li><strong>I put the new idea at the end of the sentence.</strong> Old information first, then the point.</li>
</ul>

## Resources I keep coming back to

<div class="ltw-resources">

  <div class="ltw-resource">
    <span class="ltw-resource__kind">Book</span>
    <h3><em>The Sense of Style</em></h3>
    <p class="ltw-resource__by">Steven Pinker, 2014</p>
    <p>Where I first read about the &ldquo;curse of knowledge&rdquo;. It changed how I reread my own drafts.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="ltw-resource">
    <span class="ltw-resource__kind">Article</span>
    <h3>The Science of Scientific Writing</h3>
    <p class="ltw-resource__by">George Gopen &amp; Judith Swan, <em>American Scientist</em>, 1990</p>
    <p>Taught me to think about where readers expect to find things in a sentence. I reread it before every revision.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="ltw-resource">
    <span class="ltw-resource__kind">Book</span>
    <h3><em>On Writing Well</em></h3>
    <p class="ltw-resource__by">William Zinsser</p>
    <p>My reminder that clear writing is mostly removing things.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="ltw-resource">
    <span class="ltw-resource__kind">Book</span>
    <h3><em>Writing Science</em></h3>
    <p class="ltw-resource__by">Joshua Schimel, 2012</p>
    <p>Helped me see a paper as a story, with a question that pulls the reader through.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="ltw-resource">
    <span class="ltw-resource__kind">Article</span>
    <h3>Ten simple rules for structuring papers</h3>
    <p class="ltw-resource__by">Brett Mensh &amp; Konrad Kording, <em>PLOS Computational Biology</em>, 2017</p>
    <p>The checklist I use when an outline is not working yet.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="ltw-resource">
    <span class="ltw-resource__kind">Essay</span>
    <h3>How to Write Mathematics</h3>
    <p class="ltw-resource__by">Paul Halmos, 1970</p>
    <p>Useful for the parts of my papers that are mostly notation and proofs.</p>
  </div>

</div>
