This site does not use advertising cookies, a comment system, or Google Analytics.

It counts visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not set cookies and does not identify individual visitors. Aggregate page-view statistics are public at [olicoside.goatcounter.com](https://olicoside.goatcounter.com). See [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for what that service stores.

Server access logs may record standard technical information (such as IP address, browser, and referring page) that is not used to identify you.

If you write to me, I will use your email only to reply, and I will not share it without your consent.
